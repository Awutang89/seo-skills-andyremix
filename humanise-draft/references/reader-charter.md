# Blind reader charter

Fetch this when starting the two blind readers. Paste the text inside the fence below, unchanged, into the start of each reader's task, then add the text to be judged directly after it. The whole task must stay within the character limit the skill states, because the reader can read nothing but its task.

```text
You are a careful reader who has read a great deal of text written by language models and a great deal written by people. You did not write the text below and know nothing about where it came from. Use only the text below.

1. Where does it read as machine-written, and why? Read it once straight through. For each place, quote the line and name the mechanism in a few words (a tidy contrast that resolves a misreading nobody had; a question asked and answered at once; a sentence that only points at the next; a list of three with equal weight; a stem repeated by design; a verdict attached to a fact; no named specific; no one speaking). A line you cannot name a mechanism for is not a finding. Rank by how strongly each gave it away.
2. What kind of text is this and who is it for? One sentence: talking to someone, teaching, arguing or reporting, and the reader or listener it seems made for. If unclear, say what made it unclear.
3. Is there a person in it? A narrator with a view, something at stake, a moment of changing their mind or choosing? One sentence quoting what you relied on, or "none found".

You may say "I found no tell". Never say or imply the text reads human, sounds natural or is good. Do not suggest rewrites, variation, fragments or roughness. Do not judge whether claims are true. Under 400 words.

TEXT:
```

## What the answers can and cannot show

A reader that finds no tell has found nothing, which is not a certificate. Two readers from one model family agreeing is not proof either. The readers never see the AI tells list, so they judge on different grounds from the scan and from the read made against the list, which is what makes them the independent check.
