# Article Roadmap Template

Copy this template for any niche. Fill in every field before handing to writers.
Do not start writing until the deduplication audit is complete and the cross-reference table is built.

---

## How to use this template

1. Complete the deduplication audit first (see deduplication-rules.md)
2. Fill in the Article Entry Template for every article in the database
3. Score every article using the Priority Scoring section
4. Build the Writing Roadmap table from the scores
5. Map articles to the Monthly Calendar
6. Hand roadmap + individual article entries to writers — not the full database

## Priority Scoring Sheet

Score every article before building the roadmap. Enter scores in the table below.

### Scoring criteria

**Commercial Value (1-3)**
```
3 = Direct purchase intent
    - "best X" articles
    - "X for [application]" sizing guides
    - Brand/product reviews
    - Price/cost comparison articles

2 = Research/comparison intent
    - "X vs Y" comparisons
    - "how to choose X" guides
    - "what is X" definitions for buyers
    - Application guides that lead to purchases

1 = Pure informational/authority
    - Technical specification explanations
    - Troubleshooting articles
    - Maintenance guides
    - Standards/compliance content
```

**Search Volume Potential (1-3)**
```
3 = Core category keyword (10,000+ monthly searches estimated)
    Signs: Exact match keyword, no long modifiers, people ask this constantly

2 = Strong niche keyword (1,000-10,000 monthly searches estimated)
    Signs: Has modifiers but still general, clear audience segment

1 = Longtail/specialist keyword (under 1,000 monthly searches estimated)
    Signs: Very specific, technical, or niche application
```

**Competition Opportunity (1-3)**
```
3 = High opportunity
    - Top results are thin (under 1,000 words, no depth)
    - Top results are outdated (2+ years old)
    - Exact question not answered anywhere comprehensively
    - Forums/Reddit ranking (no dedicated article exists)

2 = Medium opportunity
    - Some good content exists but gaps visible
    - 1-2 authoritative articles but several weak competitors
    - Strong content exists but on DR 20-40 sites (beatable)

1 = Low opportunity
    - Forbes, HubSpot, major trade publications rank #1-3
    - Comprehensive recent content from DR 60+ sites
    - Category is saturated with strong content
```

## Pre-Writing Checklist

Before any writer starts on an article, verify:

**Database check:**
- [ ] Primary keyword is unique in the database (not used anywhere else)
- [ ] Secondary keywords are not duplicated in other articles
- [ ] Article entry is complete (all fields filled)
- [ ] Angle note is written

**Linking check:**
- [ ] At least 3 internal links TO other articles are identified
- [ ] At least 1 internal link FROM another article is planned
- [ ] Hub article exists (if this is a spoke article)

**Writer brief:**
- [ ] Article entry handed to writer
- [ ] Angle note explained (especially what NOT to cover)
- [ ] Word count target confirmed
- [ ] Money page / CTA destination identified
- [ ] Cross-reference table available to writer (so they can add correct internal links)

---

## Post-Publishing Checklist

After an article is published:

- [ ] Update status to "Published" in the roadmap table
- [ ] Add internal links FROM this article to other published articles
- [ ] Update other articles to link TO this article where planned
- [ ] Check that no other article is targeting the same primary keyword (spot check)
- [ ] Log publication date for content refresh planning (audit every 18 months)
