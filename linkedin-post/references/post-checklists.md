# Post checklists, structures and media

The self-check lists `linkedin-post` runs before it saves. Sources and full wording are in the
`linkedin-strategy` playbook reference (sections 4, 5, 7 and 9), which is a prior: the Page records
govern voice, layout, length, links and the ask wherever they differ. The lists below are craft
prompts that raise flags for the writer and block nothing.

## The 12-point checklist (067, Di Bratto)

Mark each PASS or FLAG in the receipt.

1. Worth sharing: makes the reader look good.
2. Useful today: the reader could act on it now.
3. Worth saving: a list, process or framework with lasting value.
4. Big topic or clear outcome.
5. Answers the obvious objection inside the post.
6. Relevant media that strengthens the hook.
7. Earns "see more".
8. No filler.
9. Works for strangers (a cold reader).
10. Simple language.
11. Easy to skim.
12. Each sentence connects to the next.

## The 21 tips (079, Di Bratto)

Use as a review pass. Flag only the tips the draft breaks.

1. Write for strangers.
2. Mine real conversations, objections and problems from the role.
3. Make readers smarter, so they want to share with colleagues.
4. Work backwards from what the reader should feel or believe.
5. Add something proprietary: an insight, story, take, framework or process.
6. Package the idea before writing.
7. Spend most of the time on hook and packaging.
8. Draft five hooks and choose the best.
9. Lead with the best result.
10. Start at the most interesting part; do not bury the lead.
11. State the outcome before the steps.
12. Use specific numbers in the hook (from the facts ledger only).
13. Give away the exact process, including what a generic answer would leave out.
14. Use concrete examples.
15. Use simple words.
16. Break between ideas, not after every line.
17. Every image explains or strengthens the point.
18. Read it out loud.
19. Only as long as it needs to be.
20. The body delivers everything the hook promised.
21. Do not force a conclusion. The layout and voice records decide the close and the ask; on a
    reach post the ask may be a specific question or nothing more than the last line landing.

## Structure by post type (097, 040, 017)

Every body runs hook, context, substance, takeaway, written for a cold reader.

| Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Explainer or teach | The answer in one line, then exactly how to find and fix the problem |
| Opinion | The widely held belief most people get wrong, why the account does not believe it, what to do instead |
| Story | Bring the reader into the scene, then what it changed or taught; a real cost |
| Trend or news take | What happened, then why the ICP should care; within 48 hours of the news (098); no sales ask |
| Case study | Where the customer was stuck, what changed, what happened next, with approved numbers; the story, not only the metric (080, 098) |
| Behind the numbers | Real numbers, the mistakes behind them, what was learned |
| Objection crusher | The objection in the buyer's words, then how it is handled |
| Simulated conversation | A senior person states a misconception; a junior person challenges it and teaches what to do instead (094) |
| Timeline | Year-by-year wins and fails, skimmable (048) |
| Starting line | Where the account starts, what it will post each week, and why (048) |
| Offer breakdown | Who it is for, how the work runs, the outcome, what working together looks like (080) |

## Formatting

- Short paragraphs of one to three lines, a blank line between ideas, no line break after every
  line.
- A numbered list is fine for a quick win (058); keep items parallel but not identical in length.
- One idea per post. Two ideas are two posts.

## The ask by role word (097, 099, 024)

The layout record sets the number of asks and where links sit; the content type record sets the
job. Within them:

| Role word | Fitting ask |
|---|---|
| reach | A specific question only this post could ask, or no explicit ask |
| positioning (trust) | The strategy's lead magnet or newsletter, named in words |
| conversion (convert) | One clear next step, with the link and tags the records allow |

Never bait. Never "comment X and I will send it".

## Media matching (017, 040, 098, 037)

| Post type | Media |
|---|---|
| Founder, team or personal story | A real photograph, ideally with faces (a person supplies it) |
| Industry commentary, hot take, news take | A screenshot of the headline or post being discussed (a person captures it) |
| Dense process or framework | An infographic, one simple step-by-step image |
| Deep list | A carousel (via `content-atomizer`), used sparingly: 040 says carousels are landing poorly |
| Case study | A screenshot as proof, private details blurred |
| Tech stack roundup | Text, with the companies tagged by the person when posting |
| Opinion, simulated conversation | Text only, or a simple text card |

- Text-only is fine. No video by default (040). No talking-head video (037).
- Never a fake screenshot, dashboard, receipt or testimonial. Write a capture request: what to
  capture, what to blur.
- Zernio sends single-body posts and PDF carousels. Name the media in the receipt; a person attaches
  it.
