# Weekday templates

Background for `linkedin-weekly-plan`: the weekday templates behind a slot's funnel stage, kept to
explain a plan and to name an angle when a person asks. The skill writes the plan version from the accepted ideas, and
the Page records and the strategy govern cadence and balance, so nothing here is a rule the skill
applies. The angle (the bank type name) for each slot comes from the bank in
`references/post-type-bank.md`. The options below are the template's own suggestions, given as bank
ids. Sources and full wording: the `linkedin-strategy` playbook reference, sections 3 and 5.

## Default: the perfect week (076, Di Bratto)

The day sets the stage. The angle comes from the bank's rows for that stage. 076's own three
options per day, as bank ids:

| Day | Stage | 076 options (bank ids) |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Reach | `origin-story`, `contrarian-belief`, `hiring-opinion` |
| Tuesday | Trust | `framework`, `industry-commentary`, `big-brand-teardown` |
| Wednesday | Reach | `tech-stack-roundup`, `ai-opinion`, `mistake-story` |
| Thursday | Trust | `step-by-step`, `if-i-were`, `pro-tips` |
| Friday | Convert | `case-study`, `objection-crusher`, `sales-call-faq` |

076 runs three weeks of its 15 types before any repeats ("you just got enough for three weeks").
The plan keeps that rule across the whole bank: no angle repeats within three weeks.

## Option: Send & Sell, Plan a month (047, 103, Acosta)

Write down four client problems and run one problem through the week, one per week of the month.
Every post ends with the strategy's newsletter or lead magnet CTA (in words; link only on the
convert day).

| Day | Stage | Template (second person removed for the Page) |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Trust | "Here's how I solved [problem]" (`step-by-step`) |
| Tuesday | Reach | "[Problem] is costing [role]s [thing]" (original: "Your [problem] is costing you [thing]") (`hidden-cost`) |
| Wednesday | Convert | "I solved [problem] for [client]" (`case-study`, client only from approved ledger entries) |
| Thursday | Reach | "X years ago, I started [business]" (`origin-story`) |
| Friday | Trust | "One tip to solve [problem] in [timeframe]" (`pro-tips`) |

The stage labels for 047 and 103 are this pack's mapping; the creator gives no stages.

## Fewer than five posts

For a cadence of 2 to 4, keep the stage mix as close to the strategy's funnel mix as the count
allows, and drop days in this order: the second reach day, the second trust day. Keep the convert
day when the mix has convert above zero. Example for 3 posts with 40/40/20: reach, trust, and
convert every other week (trust in the off week). This rule is a working rule of this pack, not from the
sources.

Only when the strategy lists one. The repo's default for a weekend slot is a personal story (li-plan).

## The repo mix as a check (li-plan)

Adapted in part from the li-plan skill of `linkedin-agent-skill` by Jake Schincariol
(https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/linkedin-agent-skill), MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 Jake
Schincariol.

Across a fortnight, aim for at least one Proof (something that happened, with a number), one
Opinion (a position that could lose followers), one Teach (one thing the reader can do today), and
per fortnight one Story (a scene with dialogue and a cost) and one Offer (said plainly). Never two
of the same angle back to back.
