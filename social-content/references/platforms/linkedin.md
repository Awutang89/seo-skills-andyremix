# LinkedIn

This file covers the single feed post, optionally with one image. Multi-slide document posts are a separate format; long-form Articles and Newsletters are covered by content-atomizer.

## Best fit

- business context
- lessons from real work
- stories backed by proof
- operational examples
- considered opinions with something specific at stake

## Hard limits

- Under 3,000 characters.
- Usual target 500 to 1,200 characters, when the proof supports that much.
- Hashtags only when the person's examples or the brief use them.
- No engagement bait.
- A link goes in the post itself when the person supplies one and wants it there. Do not move it to the comments as a reach trick.

## Shape

- Open on a specific tension, scene or proof point.
- Say why the reader should care.
- Give enough professional context to make it relevant.
- Short paragraphs, usually one or two sentences.
- Line breaks for readability, not one-sentence-per-line "broetry".
- End on a grounded takeaway or a pointed last sentence.

## Proof

LinkedIn carries more context than X or Threads, and the honesty bar is the same:

- each number tied to the right entity
- causal claims qualified when the proof is limited
- no invented client quotes or unnamed "someone told me" scenes
- the business consequence stated plainly, without overclaiming

## Failure modes

- throat-clearing before the proof
- generic consultant framing
- "here are 5 lessons" when a story would carry it better
- polished voice that ignores the real examples
- motivational closers in place of a useful last line
