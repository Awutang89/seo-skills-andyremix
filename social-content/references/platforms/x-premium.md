# X Premium (long posts)

Use only when the person has long-post access, the saved profile says so, or they asked for a long X post. Never switch here because a draft is hard to squeeze under 280. Status unknown: ask, or use `x.md`.

Allowed to be long does not mean supposed to be long. Stay compact unless the story, the proof, the argument or the nuance truly needs the room. Never aim at the ceiling.

## Best fit

- short essays led by proof
- nuanced takes that need their caveats
- quick teardowns or walkthroughs
- first-person stories that arrive at a point
- posts where the first 280 characters hook and the rest pays off the click

## When to ask about access

Ask (or read it from the profile) when:

- the person posts to X often
- they ask for a long X post
- the draft naturally runs past 280
- a reusable profile is being built or refreshed

Unknown stays standard X.

## Limits

- X's own help pages have described long posts for Premium subscribers of up to 25,000 characters. That figure came from the source pack without a date; platform limits move, so re-check it and treat the saved profile as the truth for this person.
- The hook and the main point sit inside the first 280 characters, and those 280 stand on their own. `scripts/check_post.py post --platform x-premium` reports whether the first 280 weighted characters end mid-sentence.
- No hashtags unless the examples use them.
- No engagement bait.

## Shape

- Opens like X, not like LinkedIn.
- Extra space goes to proof, nuance, a worked example or a sharper argument.
- Every paragraph past 280 has to earn its place.
- Short paragraphs; get from claim to evidence fast.
- Never stretch a 280-character idea into an essay.

## Prefer standard X when

- the idea fits in 280
- the person wants a quick take
- the proof is thin
- the long version would turn into filler
- they publish through a third-party tool that may still cap posts at 280

## Failure modes

- a LinkedIn essay pasted into X
- length used to hide a weak point
- the hook buried under setup
- a simple take over-explained
- Premium assumed without asking or checking the profile
