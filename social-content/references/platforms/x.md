# X (standard)

## Best fit

- compressed takes
- sharp contrasts
- proof snippets
- short story beats
- pointed observations

## Hard limits

- Under 280 characters unless an X thread was requested.
- Aim for 260 or fewer, so a last-minute tweak cannot tip it over.
- Count the final text with `scripts/check_post.py post --platform x` (it weights characters the way X does: links count 23, most emoji and CJK count 2). Never guess a count or report one that was not checked.
- Over 280: cut context, adjectives and the second example before anything else.
- One post, unless alternatives or a thread were asked for.
- No hashtags unless the person's examples use them.
- No "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment below" or other bait closers.

## Shape

- Skip the setup.
- Open on the tension or the proof.
- Make one point.
- Plain sentence rhythm.
- End on a grounded line, not a call to action.

## X threads

Only when requested. When writing one:

- Every post moves the idea forward.
- Never slice a LinkedIn draft into pieces.
- Each post reads on its own.
- Proof limits hold in every post, not just the first.
- A thread earns its form when each post depends on the one before; independent points are better as separate posts.
- Separate posts with a line containing only `---` so the lint and any scheduler can split them.

## Failure modes

- more context than the feed will read
- generic "lesson" framing
- a LinkedIn post trimmed to fit
- a fake aphorism in place of a real point
- broad hashtags or bait
